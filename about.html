About This Course

The Course

FR 365 — Masterworks of French Literature is offered at Loyola University Maryland in the Fall 2026 semester. The course focuses on Victor Hugo's Les Misérables as a lens through which to develop skills in close reading, historical contextualization, and literary analysis. Students engage the text in translation and in the original, attending to both its literary craft and its historical moment.

The Book

Published in 1862, Les Misérables is one of the great novels of the nineteenth century. Set in post-Napoleonic France, it follows the ex-convict Jean Valjean across decades of social upheaval, tracing the lives of the poor, the criminal, the revolutionary, and the redeemed. Hugo weaves together questions of justice, mercy, poverty, and moral transformation into a work of enormous scope and enduring power. It remains one of the most widely read novels in the world.
